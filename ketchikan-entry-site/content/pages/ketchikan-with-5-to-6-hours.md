# Ketchikan With 5–6 Hours

This is a **short Ketchikan stop**.

You must account for:

- Dock shuttle time
- Rain delays
- Reboarding cutoff

---

## Safe Choices

- Totem village tours
- Short wildlife viewing
- Downtown + Creek Street

---

## High-Risk Choices

- Misty Fjords flightseeing
- Long fishing charters
- Multi-stop combos

Short Ketchikan days reward simplicity.
