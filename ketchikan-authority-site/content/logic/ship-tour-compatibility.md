# Ship ↔ Tour Compatibility

This matrix shows which tour categories are **safe**, **conditional**, or **not recommended**
based on **port call length**, **arrival time**, and **weather sensitivity**.

---

## Legend

- ✅ **Safe** – High confidence fit
- ⚠️ **Conditional** – Depends on timing or weather
- ❌ **Not Recommended** – High risk of missed departure

---

## Compatibility Matrix

| Ship Arrival Window  | Port Call Length | Flightseeing | Wildlife Tours | Rail Tours | Short Ground Tours |
| -------------------- | ---------------- | ------------ | -------------- | ---------- | ------------------ |
| Early (06:00–08:00)  | Long (8–10h)     | ✅           | ✅             | ✅         | ✅                 |
| Early (06:00–08:00)  | Short (4–6h)     | ⚠️           | ⚠️             | ❌         | ✅                 |
| Midday (09:00–11:00) | Long (8–10h)     | ⚠️           | ✅             | ⚠️         | ✅                 |
| Midday (09:00–11:00) | Short (4–6h)     | ❌           | ⚠️             | ❌         | ⚠️                 |
| Late (12:00+)        | Any              | ❌           | ❌             | ❌         | ⚠️                 |

---

## Notes

- **Flightseeing** is highly weather-dependent and should never be booked without buffer time.
- **Rail tours** require fixed departure windows and are risky on short port calls.
- **Short ground tours** are the safest fallback across all ports.
