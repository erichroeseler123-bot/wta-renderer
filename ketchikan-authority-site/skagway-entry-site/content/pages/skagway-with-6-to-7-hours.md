# Skagway With 6–7 Hours

Six to seven hours in Skagway is workable — but not flexible.

You should assume **one major activity only**.

---

## Best Choices

- White Pass Railroad (short or mid-length option)
- Downtown Skagway exploration
- Dyea Valley scenic drive

---

## What to Avoid

- Long Yukon loops
- Multi-stop adventure combos
- Anything dependent on border wait times

If your ship departs early afternoon, margin matters more than ambition.
