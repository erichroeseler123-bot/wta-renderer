---
title: "Rainy Days in Juneau: What Still Works"
description: "Excursions and activities that remain feasible during rain in Juneau."
---

Rain is normal in Juneau and rarely stops all activity.

## Rain-Resilient Options

- Whale watching
- Mendenhall Visitor Center
- Downtown walking and museums
- Scenic drives

## Rain-Sensitive Activities

- Glacier hiking
- Kayaking
- Helicopter tours (visibility-dependent)

## Key Insight

Rain alone is rarely the problem. Wind and fog are the real constraints.
