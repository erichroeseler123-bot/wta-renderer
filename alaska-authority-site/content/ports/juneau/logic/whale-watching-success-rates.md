Juneau is one of the most reliable whale-watching ports in Alaska.

## Typical Success Rates

- Peak season (June–August): 90–95%
- Shoulder season: 80–90%

## Influencing Factors

- Tide cycles
- Feeding patterns
- Vessel type
- Time of day

## Guarantees Explained

“Guaranteed sightings” often mean:

- Return trip offered
- Partial refund
- Credit toward future tour

Weather rarely stops whales — it stops boats.

This page exists to set expectations, not sell tours.
