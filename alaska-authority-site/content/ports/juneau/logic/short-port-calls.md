Many Juneau cruise stops are shorter than passengers expect once disembarkation, transportation, and return buffers are accounted for.

If your total **usable time ashore is under 6 hours**, your options narrow fast.

## What Works Reliably

- Whale watching tours under 3.5 hours
- Mendenhall Glacier shuttle visits (no guided hikes)
- Downtown walking and food stops
- Small-group excursions with guaranteed return policies

## What Usually Fails

- Helicopter + dogsled combos
- Multi-activity tours
- Anything requiring weather-dependent flights

## Key Reality

Cruise lines pad itineraries optimistically. Independent planning should assume **1–1.5 hours lost** to logistics.

If your ship departure is before 5:00 PM, prioritize **one anchor activity only**.

This page focuses on feasibility — not marketing promises.
