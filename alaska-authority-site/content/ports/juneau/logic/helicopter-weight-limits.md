Helicopter tours in Juneau operate under **strict FAA weight and balance rules**.

This affects far more passengers than brochures suggest.

## Common Limits

- Standard max per passenger: 250 lbs
- Above limit: 1.5× pricing or denial
- Exact weight required day-of

## Why This Matters

One overweight passenger can:

- Delay the entire group
- Reduce fuel range
- Cause last-minute cancellation

## Best Advice

If anyone in your party may exceed limits:

- Confirm exact operator policies
- Book refundable options
- Consider non-flight alternatives

This is a **logistics reality**, not a judgment.
