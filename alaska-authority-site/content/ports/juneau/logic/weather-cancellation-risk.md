Juneau weather is not an exception — it is the rule.

Rain, fog, and wind directly impact excursion feasibility.

## Highest Risk Activities

- Helicopter glacier landings
- Flightseeing tours
- Seaplane excursions

## Medium Risk

- Glacier hiking
- Kayaking
- Small-boat whale tours

## Lowest Risk

- Large vessel whale watching
- Mendenhall Visitor Center visits
- Downtown walking and museums

## Reality Check

Most weather cancellations occur **morning-of**, not the night before.

Passengers should prioritize tours with:

- Full refunds
- Ship-return guarantees
- Same-day rescheduling

This guide exists to reduce missed ports and lost money.
