---
title: "Glacier Access in Juneau Without Helicopters"
description: "How cruise passengers can see glaciers in Juneau without flightseeing."
---

Not all glacier experiences require helicopters.

## Ground-Based Options

- Mendenhall Glacier Visitor Center
- Nugget Falls trail
- Bus and shuttle excursions

## Boat-Based Alternatives

- Fjord cruises (longer port calls only)

## Tradeoff

You lose icefield landings but gain reliability.

For many passengers, ground access is the smarter choice.
