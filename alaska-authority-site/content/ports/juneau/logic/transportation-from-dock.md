---
title: "Transportation From the Cruise Dock in Juneau"
description: "How far excursions actually are from Juneau cruise docks and why timing matters."
---

Juneau excursions are rarely walking-distance from cruise docks.

## Typical Distances

- Mendenhall Glacier: 12–13 miles
- Auke Bay whale tours: 15 miles
- Helicopter bases: 10–20 minutes by road

## Hidden Time Cost

- Bus staging
- Traffic during peak ship overlap
- Late-return buffer enforcement

## Smart Planning

Assume **45–60 minutes round-trip** transportation time minimum.

Short port calls magnify transportation errors.

Distance, not price, is the limiting factor.
