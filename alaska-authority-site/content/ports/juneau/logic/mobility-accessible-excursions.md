---
title: "Mobility-Accessible Excursions in Juneau"
description: "What works for cruise passengers with limited mobility."
---

Juneau offers several low-impact excursion options.

## Most Accessible Choices

- Whale watching boats
- Mendenhall Visitor Center
- Scenic drives

## Less Accessible

- Glacier hiking
- Kayaking
- Helicopter landings (steep steps)

Always confirm vehicle and vessel accessibility in advance.
