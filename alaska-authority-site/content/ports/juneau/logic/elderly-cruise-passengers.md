title: "Juneau Excursions for Elderly Cruise Passengers"
description: "Practical excursion guidance for older travelers."
---

Juneau excursions vary widely in physical demand.

## Recommended

- Large vessel whale tours
- Scenic transportation-based tours
- Visitor centers

## Use Caution With

- Steep trails
- Ice walking
- Small aircraft

Fatigue compounds quickly in cold, wet weather.
