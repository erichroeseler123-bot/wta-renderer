---
title: "Long Port Calls in Juneau: How to Use the Time Well"
description: "What becomes feasible in Juneau when your ship is docked 8 hours or more."
---

Long port calls open up options that are impossible on shorter stops.

## What Becomes Possible

- Glacier trekking
- Helicopter glacier landings
- Combination whale + glacier tours
- Longer fjord cruises

## Still Requires Caution

Longer time does not remove weather risk. Helicopters and boats can still cancel.

## Best Strategy

Book one primary excursion and leave buffer time for delays.

Long port calls reward ambition — but only with margin.
