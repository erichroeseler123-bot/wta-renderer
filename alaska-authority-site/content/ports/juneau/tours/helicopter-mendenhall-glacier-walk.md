Helicopter glacier walk tours from Juneau typically include ground transfers, a safety briefing, a round-trip helicopter flight, and a guided walk on glacial ice.

---

## Typical Duration

- **Total time:** 2.5–3.5 hours
- **Flight time:** ~25–30 minutes round-trip
- **On-glacier time:** 20–60 minutes

Additional buffer time is required for cruise disembarkation and re-boarding.

---

## Requirements

- Minimum age varies by operator (commonly 5–7+)
- Guests must be able to walk on uneven ice
- Weight disclosures required for aircraft balance

---

## Cruise Compatibility Summary

These tours generally require a **minimum net shore window of 6.5–7 hours** to be considered feasible for cruise passengers.

Compatibility depends on ship size, berth location, and weather conditions.
