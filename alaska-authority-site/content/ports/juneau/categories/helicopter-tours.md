Helicopter tours are one of the most memorable experiences available in Juneau — but they are also the **most misunderstood and most frequently canceled** excursion type for cruise passengers.

This page explains **how Juneau helicopter tours really work**, who they are best for, and when they are _not_ a smart choice during a cruise stop.

## Are Helicopter Tours Worth It in Juneau?

Yes — **if your ship schedule and weather align**.

Juneau sits directly beneath the Juneau Icefield, making it one of the few places in North America where glaciers are accessible by helicopter within minutes of town. Flights typically land on remote icefields or glaciers such as Herbert or Mendenhall.

However, helicopter tours are:

- Highly weather-dependent
- Strict on weight limits
- Vulnerable to cruise port delays

## Common Reasons Helicopter Tours Cancel

Cruise passengers are often surprised by last-minute cancellations. The most common causes are:

- Low cloud ceilings over the icefield
- Fog forming rapidly over coastal terrain
- High winds at glacier landing zones
- Aircraft weight and balance restrictions

Even clear skies in downtown Juneau **do not guarantee** flyable glacier conditions.

## Who Should Book a Helicopter Tour?

Helicopter tours work best for:

- Passengers with **long port calls (6+ hours)**
- Ships arriving early in the morning
- Guests comfortable with uncertainty and rescheduling
- Travelers prioritizing scenery over guaranteed completion

They are _not_ ideal for:

- Tight schedules
- Passengers with mobility limitations
- Anyone uncomfortable with weather risk

## Glacier Landings and Activities

Most helicopter tours include:

- A scenic flight over the Tongass rainforest
- A glacier landing
- A short guided walk on the ice

More advanced options may include:

- Glacier trekking
- Ice climbing
- Dog sledding camps (seasonal)

## Bottom Line

Helicopter tours in Juneau can be extraordinary — but they are not a “sure thing.”

If conditions align, they deliver one of the most dramatic Alaska experiences possible. If conditions don’t, cancellations are common and unavoidable.

Understanding this tradeoff before booking is essential for a good cruise experience.
