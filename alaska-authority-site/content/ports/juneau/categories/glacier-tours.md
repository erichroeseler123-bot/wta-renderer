Glacier tours are the backbone of Juneau tourism, but not all glacier experiences are the same.

From roadside viewpoints to helicopter landings, this page explains the **real differences** between glacier tour options and which ones make sense during a cruise stop.

## Mendenhall Glacier Overview

Mendenhall Glacier is:

- Located 12 miles from downtown Juneau
- The only glacier in Southeast Alaska accessible by road
- A popular stop for cruise excursions and independent travelers

Most visitors experience Mendenhall via:

- Shuttle or bus tours
- Independent transport
- Combo tours with whale watching

## Bus and Shuttle Glacier Tours

These tours typically include:

- Round-trip transport
- Time at the visitor center
- Access to nearby trails and viewpoints

They are:

- Reliable
- Low risk
- Suitable for all mobility levels

## Boat-Based Glacier Tours

Some full-day tours travel deeper into fjords such as Tracy Arm, where tidewater glaciers calve into the sea.

These tours:

- Require long port calls
- Are more weather-sensitive
- Offer dramatic scenery and wildlife viewing

## Helicopter Glacier Access

Helicopters allow direct access to remote icefields but come with:

- Higher cost
- Weight limits
- Weather dependency

They offer the most immersive glacier experience but carry the highest risk of cancellation.

## Bottom Line

For cruise passengers:

- Mendenhall Glacier tours are the safest and easiest option
- Boat-based glacier tours require careful schedule planning
- Helicopter access offers unmatched views but real risk

Choosing the right glacier experience depends on **time, weather tolerance, and expectations**.
