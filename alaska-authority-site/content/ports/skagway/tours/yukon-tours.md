---
title: Yukon Tours from Skagway
description: Feasibility and risks of Yukon highway and border-crossing tours from Skagway.
---

Yukon tours are appealing — but they are **not low-risk excursions** during a cruise stop.

## What Makes Them Risky

- Border crossing delays
- Long drive times
- Weather in mountain passes
- Tight ship departure windows

## When They Work

- Long port calls (7+ hours)
- Early departures
- Ship-sponsored tours with return guarantees

## When They Don’t

- Short port calls
- Independent operators without buffers
- Afternoon departures

Always evaluate timing before booking anything involving Canada.
