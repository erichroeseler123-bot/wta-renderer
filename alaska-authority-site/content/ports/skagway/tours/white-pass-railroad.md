description: What to know before booking the White Pass & Yukon Route Railroad during a Skagway cruise stop.

The White Pass & Yukon Route Railroad is the most popular excursion in Skagway — and also the most capacity-constrained.

## Why It’s Popular

- Direct access from the dock
- Minimal walking
- Scenic mountain views
- Predictable duration

## Real Constraints

- Limited daily departures
- Tickets sell out early
- Late departures risk tight returns

Before booking, review these planning realities:

- Train schedule limitations
- Short port call feasibility
- Return-to-ship timing risks
