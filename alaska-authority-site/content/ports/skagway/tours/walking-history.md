title: Walking and History Tours in Skagway
description: Low-risk walking and historical activities near the Skagway cruise dock.
---

Skagway’s historic district is immediately adjacent to the cruise docks, making walking tours the **lowest-risk** option.

## Why These Work Well

- No transportation required
- Flexible timing
- Easy early return to ship
- Suitable for short port calls

## Typical Options

- Broadway historic walking tours
- National Park Service talks
- Museums and exhibits
- Self-guided exploration

These are ideal backup plans when weather or timing limits larger excursions.
