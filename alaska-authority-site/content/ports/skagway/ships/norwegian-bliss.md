Norwegian Bliss is one of the largest ships to call on Skagway, which creates unique congestion and timing constraints.

## What Matters Most

- Train tickets sell out early
- Large passenger volume causes delays
- Independent Yukon tours carry return risk

## Best Options

- Ship-sponsored White Pass Railroad
- Early departure train loops
- Walking-distance activities on Broadway

## Avoid

- Full Yukon loops unless ship-backed
- Late-afternoon departures with border crossings
