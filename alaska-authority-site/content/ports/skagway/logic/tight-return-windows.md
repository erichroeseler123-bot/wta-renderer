## The Reality in Skagway

Ships leave exactly on time.

## What Actually Works

- Excursions with buffer time
- Staying within town late day

## What Usually Fails

- Back-to-back tours
- Border or highway routes near departure

## Bottom Line

Always plan to be back early, not on time.
