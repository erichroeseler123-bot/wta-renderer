## The Reality in Skagway

Train departures are fixed and sell out early.

## What Actually Works

- Booking early morning departures
- Choosing shorter routes on busy days

## What Usually Fails

- Same-day onboard bookings
- Expecting flexible return times

## Bottom Line

Train timing controls your whole day — plan around it.
