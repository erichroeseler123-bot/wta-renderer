Ketchikan is known as the totem capital of the world, and many cultural sites are close to port.

## Why These Work

- Short travel times
- Reliable scheduling
- Low physical demand

## Typical Stops

- Totem Bight State Park
- Saxman Village
- Local museums

These are excellent options for tight schedules or mobility concerns.
