Misty Fjords is one of the most visually dramatic excursions in Southeast Alaska — but access method matters.

## Tour Types

- Floatplane flightseeing
- Boat-based fjord cruises
- Kayak excursions (weather dependent)

## Real Constraints

- Weather visibility
- Flight cancellations
- Long durations vs short port calls

These tours work best with early departures and backup plans.
