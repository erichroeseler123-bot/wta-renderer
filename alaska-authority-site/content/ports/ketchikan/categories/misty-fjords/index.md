Misty Fjords offers some of the most dramatic scenery in Southeast Alaska — but it is also **one of the highest-risk excursions** from a cruise port.

Most access routes depend on **floatplanes or long boat transits**, both of which are highly weather sensitive.

## What Works Best

- Morning floatplane departures
- Clear ceiling forecasts at booking time
- Longer port calls with buffer time

## High-Risk Factors

- Low cloud ceilings
- Rain and fog stacking throughout the day
- Booking Misty Fjords as a second activity

## Who Should Consider It

- Experienced Alaska cruisers
- Guests comfortable with weather cancellations
- Travelers prioritizing scenery over certainty

Misty Fjords is incredible when conditions align — but always plan a **backup option** in Ketchikan.
