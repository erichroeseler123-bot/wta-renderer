Floatplane tours are iconic in Ketchikan — and also among the **most weather-dependent** excursions in Alaska.

Ceiling height, fog, and rain directly determine whether flights operate.

## Best Practices

- Book the earliest available departure
- Avoid stacking floatplanes with other tours
- Monitor weather closely the night before arrival

## Common Reasons for Cancellation

- Low cloud ceilings
- Persistent rain bands
- Marine layer fog

## Best Fit For

- Long port calls
- Flexible travelers
- Guests with no hard follow-up plans

If a floatplane tour is important to you, treat it as a **priority activity**, not an add-on.
