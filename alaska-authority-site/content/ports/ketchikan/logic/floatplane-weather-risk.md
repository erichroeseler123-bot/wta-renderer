## The Reality in Ketchikan

Cloud ceilings cancel flights more often than rain.

## What Actually Works

- Early-day flights
- Flexible itineraries

## What Usually Fails

- Afternoon departures
- Tight ship return windows

## Bottom Line

Never plan floatplanes late in the day.
